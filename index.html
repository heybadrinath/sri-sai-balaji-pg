BEFORE YOU MOVE IN

Good questions.
Clear answers.

Still wondering about something?
Ask us on WhatsApp ↗

Is there a curfew?

You can come and go 24/7. Biometric access at the entrance keeps entry secure.

What are the notice and deposit policies?

There is a standard one-month notice period before vacating. Contact us for the deposit amount and the return terms before you move in.

Can friends or family visit?

Family and friends are welcome in the visitor zones. Male visitors are restricted to the reception lounge.

How often are rooms cleaned?

Our in-house housekeeping team cleans rooms and washrooms every day.

Can I work or study from here?

Yes. There is high-speed commercial Wi-Fi across the rooms and floors. Study tables and chairs are available in 2-sharing rooms only.

Can I cook my own food?

Yes, there's a dedicated self-cooking kitchen. Breakfast, lunch and dinner are also provided daily, along with RO drinking water and a shared refrigerator.
